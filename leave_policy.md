# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; any remaining days expire on March 31. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave each year. A medical certificate is required only when sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months.

## Public Holidays
Northwind Labs observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published each December on the intranet.
