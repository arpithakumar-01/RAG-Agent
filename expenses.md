# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with itemized receipts. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless approved by the finance director.

## Approval Limits
Claims up to 25,000 rupees are approved by the employee's manager. Claims above 25,000 rupees also require approval from a director. Claims above 100,000 rupees require approval from the CFO.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours with director approval. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day. Hotel bookings must use the company travel portal.
