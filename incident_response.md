# Incident Response Guide

## Severity Levels
SEV1 is a full outage or data breach affecting customers. SEV2 is a major feature degraded for many customers. SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround available.

## Response Times
For a SEV1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge within 15 minutes and an incident commander is assigned immediately. SEV2 incidents must be acknowledged within 1 hour. SEV3 incidents are handled the next business day.

## On-Call Rotation
The on-call rotation changes weekly every Monday at 10:00 AM. Engineers on call receive a stipend and one compensatory day off after a week of paging. Engineers may swap shifts with at least 24 hours of notice.

## Communication and Postmortems
During a SEV1 incident the status page must be updated every 30 minutes until resolution. A blameless postmortem must be published within 5 business days of any SEV1 or SEV2 incident, including a timeline, root cause, and action items with owners.
