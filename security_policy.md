# Northwind Labs Security Policy

## Passwords and Authentication
All employees must use passwords of at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, and every production system. Hardware security keys are issued to engineers with production access.

## Device Security
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not be used to access customer data.

## Phishing and Reporting
Suspected phishing emails must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of receipt. Do not forward the suspicious email to colleagues. Employees who click a suspicious link should change their password immediately and notify the security team.

## Production Access
Access to production systems requires approval from two people: the employee's manager and a member of the security team. Production access is reviewed every quarter and removed automatically if it has not been used in 60 days.
