# New Hire Onboarding

## First Day
New employees receive their laptop and accounts on the first day. Each new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy who answers questions during the first 90 days. The first day includes a welcome session with the People team at 10:00 AM.

## First Two Weeks
Mandatory security awareness training and the code of conduct course must be completed within the first 2 weeks. Engineers also complete the production safety course before receiving production access.

## 30-60-90 Plan
Managers agree a 30-60-90 day plan with each new hire in the first week. Progress is reviewed in one-on-one meetings at day 30, day 60, and day 90. Probation ends after a successful day 90 review.
